For questions, feedback, and support regarding the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook, please use the appropriate channels below.

Getting Help#

Documentation#

Before contacting support, please check our comprehensive documentation:

  • About - Information about the textbook
  • Assessments - Evaluation materials and exercises
  • Hardware Requirements - System requirements for projects
  • Welcome - Getting started guide

Community Support#

  • Discussion Forum: GitHub Discussions - Ask questions and share experiences with other learners
  • Chat: Discord Server - Real-time support and community interaction
  • Q&A: Stack Overflow - Tag questions with physical-ai-textbook

Contact Channels#

General Inquiries#

For general questions about the textbook content, course structure, or educational approach:

  • Email: textbook@physical-ai.org
  • Response Time: 2-3 business days

Technical Support#

For technical issues with installations, code examples, or system configurations:

  • GitHub Issues: Submit an Issue
  • Response Time: 1-2 business days for critical issues
  • Issue Template: Please use the appropriate template for your issue type

Academic Support#

For educators using this textbook in courses or researchers collaborating on robotics projects:

  • Academic Email: academic@physical-ai.org
  • Response Time: 1 business day
  • Resources: Specialized documentation for course integration

Commercial Inquiries#

For licensing, enterprise use, or commercial partnership opportunities:

  • Business Email: business@physical-ai.org
  • Response Time: 1-2 business days
  • Support: Custom solutions and enterprise support options

Contributing to the Textbook#

Content Contributions#

We welcome contributions to improve the textbook content:

  1. Fork the Repository: GitHub Repository
  2. Create a Branch: Use descriptive branch names
  3. Submit a Pull Request: Follow our contribution guidelines
  4. Review Process: All contributions are reviewed by the editorial team

Reporting Issues#

Help improve the textbook by reporting issues:

  • Content Errors: Factual inaccuracies, outdated information, or unclear explanations
  • Technical Issues: Code examples that don't work, broken links, or formatting problems
  • Feature Requests: Suggestions for new content or improvements

Suggesting Improvements#

  • New Chapters: Propose new content areas or advanced topics
  • Updated Examples: Modernize code examples with newer technologies
  • Additional Exercises: Contribute new hands-on exercises and projects

Office Hours & Events#

Live Q&A Sessions#

Regular live sessions for real-time support:

  • Schedule: Every Wednesday at 15:00 UTC
  • Platform: Zoom (link provided via email)
  • Registration: Required for capacity management
  • Topics: Current module content, project assistance, advanced topics

Virtual Office Hours#

  • Schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 14:00-16:00 UTC
  • Format: One-on-one or small group sessions
  • Booking: Through our scheduling system
  • Topics: Personal project guidance, concept clarification

Workshops & Webinars#

  • Monthly Workshops: Deep-dive sessions on specific topics
  • Guest Lectures: Industry experts sharing practical insights
  • Project Showcases: Student and community project presentations

Stay Connected#

Newsletter#

Subscribe to our newsletter for updates:

  • Monthly Updates: New content, bug fixes, and feature releases
  • Industry News: Relevant developments in robotics and AI
  • Community Highlights: Featured projects and community contributions
  • Subscribe: GitHub Repository

Social Media#

Follow us for real-time updates and community content:

  • Twitter: @PhysicalAIBook
  • LinkedIn: Physical AI Textbook
  • YouTube: Physical AI Channel
  • GitHub: Physical AI Book

Events & Conferences#

We regularly participate in robotics and AI conferences:

  • ROSCon: Annual ROS community conference
  • ICRA: International Conference on Robotics and Automation
  • IROS: International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems
  • Local Meetups: Regional robotics and AI community events

Feedback & Surveys#

Your feedback helps us improve the textbook:

  • Course Feedback: End-of-module surveys to improve content
  • Usability Studies: Opportunities to participate in user experience research
  • Beta Testing: Early access to new content and features
  • Instructor Feedback: Specialized surveys for educators using the textbook

Emergency Support#

For critical issues affecting your learning or project work:

  • Emergency Email: emergency@physical-ai.org
  • Priority: Issues affecting course deadlines or major projects
  • Response Time: 4 hours during business days
  • Criteria: Documentation required to verify urgency

Thank you for using the Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook. We're committed to providing excellent support to help you succeed in your robotics journey!
